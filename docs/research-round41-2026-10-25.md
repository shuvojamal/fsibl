# Research round 41: picking "quality" DSE shares in advance

**Owner's examples** (price + bonus, cash dividends excluded, to Jan 2026;
`research/results/research41.out` for the screen, single-share figures from
the same data):

| share | 5 years | 10 years | 13 years | worst drop 2016-26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kohinoor | +12.0% | +16.3% | +19.6% | −40% |
| BRAC Bank | +14.5% | +14.4% | +16.4% | about −60% (data shows −69%, partly a missed bonus) |
| Uttara Bank | +13.1% | +10.3% | +5.5% | −45% |
| Ibn Sina | +5.5% | +10.0% | +14.6% | −37% |
| Marico | +5.2% | +7.3% | +15.8% | −33% |
| Square Pharma | −1.1% | +1.9% | +9.0% | −45% |
| DSEX | −2.7% | +0.9% | +1.8% | −43% |

With cash dividends added (not in the data; these companies typically pay
2-8% a year) their total returns are higher still. As an equal-weight basket
held since 2013 they made +14.8%/yr (2013-19) and +11.8%/yr (2020-26),
against about +2% for DSEX. But they were picked today, knowing how they did.

**Test.** A quality screen using only past prices (5-year growth, number of
up years in the last five, shallow 3-year drop), top 10 of the 100 most
traded established shares, rebalanced yearly, 0.7% costs per side. Pass bar
(set first): beat DSEX in both halves with a worst drop within 5 points.

| | 2013-10..2019 | 2020..2026-01 |
|---|---|---|
| quality top 10, yearly (primary) | −5.7%/yr | −0.5%/yr |
| quality top 5 | −3.9% | +0.9% |
| quality top 20 | −6.8% | −5.6% |
| quality top 10, 6-monthly | −7.4% | −4.5% |
| owner's six (hindsight) | +14.8% | +11.8% |
| DSEX | +2.1% | +2.4% |

**Verdict: fails.** The screen did hold the owner's kind of shares often
(BRAC Bank, Ibn Sina, Square, Uttara Bank, Kohinoor were among its most
frequent picks) but also shares with similar past charts that then fell
(PTL, Beacon Pharma, FineFoods, several insurers). Past price behaviour
alone can't separate the lasting winners from the rest; what made the
owner's six last was the businesses (earnings, dividends, management),
which this data doesn't contain.

**Meaning.** Choosing DSE shares like these is fundamental investing, a
judgement on the companies. A price-based signal bot can't do that job.
